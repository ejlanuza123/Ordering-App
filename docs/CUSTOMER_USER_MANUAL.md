# Customer Mobile User Manual — Petron San Pedro Mobile App

Welcome to the Customer User Manual for the **Petron San Pedro Mobile Application**. This guide will help you order fuel, lubricants, engine oils, and auto accessories with real-time tracking, 1-tap re-ordering, delivery timeline monitoring, and operational status awareness.

---

## 1. Before You Start

- Install or open the production mobile app on your Android or iOS device.
- Turn on your device's Internet connection (Wi-Fi or Mobile Data).
- **Allow Notification Permissions**: Essential for real-time order updates, rider arrival alerts, and live chat notifications.
- **Allow Location Access**: Recommended for instant GPS address detection and pin placement.

---

## 2. Account Registration & Login

### A. Register a New Account
1. Open the app and tap **Register**.
2. Enter your Full Name, Email, Password, Phone Number, and Delivery Address.
3. Tap **Sign Up**.
4. Log in using your new email and password.

### B. Sign In
1. Enter your registered email and password.
2. Tap **Sign In**.
3. The app will authenticate and load the Store Home screen with shimmer skeleton loading.

---

## 3. Store Status & Operational Advisories

The app keeps you informed of station operating status and weather emergencies in real time:

- **🟢 Normal Operations**: Station is open for instant ordering and immediate delivery dispatch.
- **🔴 Emergency Advisory & Holiday Notice**:
  - A pulsing announcement banner appears on the **Home**, **Cart**, and **Checkout** screens detailing the reason (e.g. *Severe Tropical Storm Advisory*).
  - If **Pre-Orders are Enabled**: You can still browse and submit a scheduled pre-order for priority fulfillment when the station reopens.
  - If **Pre-Orders are Disabled**: The Checkout button displays `STORE PAUSED` to prevent premature order placement.
- **🔵 Full-Screen Maintenance Lock**:
  - During scheduled system upgrades, the app displays a full-screen maintenance notice with a **Live Reopening Countdown** (`Xh Ym Zs`).
  - When the timer expires, the app automatically unlocks without requiring you to restart.

---

## 4. Browsing & Placing an Order

1. On the Home screen, browse products by category (`Fuel`, `Motor Oil`, `Engine Oil`, `Accessories`).
2. Tap any product to view details, technical specifications, stock availability, and unit price.
3. Select your desired quantity and tap **Add to Cart**.
4. Open **Cart** (`🛒` icon in top bar).
5. Review items, quantities, and subtotal.
6. Tap **Proceed to Checkout**.
7. Choose your Delivery Address:
   - **Manual Address Entry**: Type your complete delivery street address.
   - **Current GPS Location**: Tap **Use Current Location** to auto-detect coordinates.
   - **Pin Location on Map**: Drag the map pin to set your exact drop-off spot.
8. Select Payment Method (`Cash on Delivery` or `GCash`).
9. Add optional Delivery Notes (e.g. "Gate 2 near landmark").
10. Tap **Place Order**.

---

## 5. ⚡ 1-Tap Re-Order (Fast Checkout)

Repeat past orders in seconds without browsing individual products:

1. Go to the **Order History** tab.
2. Locate any past completed or delivered order.
3. Tap the **`⚡ Re-Order`** pill button directly on the order card (or tap **`⚡ Re-Order Items in 1-Tap`** inside Order Details).
4. All products from that order are instantly added to your shopping cart with proper quantities.
5. Tap **Cart** ➔ **Checkout** to complete your repeat order immediately!

---

## 6. 🚚 Live 5-Step Delivery Status Timeline & GPS Tracking

Track your delivery in real-time step-by-step:

1. Open **Order History**.
2. Tap your active order.
3. View the **Live 5-Step Delivery Timeline**:
   - Step 1: **Pending** ⏳ (Order submitted to station)
   - Step 2: **Processing** ⚙️ (Station preparing order items)
   - Step 3: **Rider Picked Up** 📦 (Assigned rider collected items)
   - Step 4: **Out for Delivery** 🚚 (Rider traveling to your address)
   - Step 5: **Completed** ✅ (Order delivered)
4. **Assigned Rider Details**: View your rider's name, vehicle plate number, phone number, and star rating.
5. **Interactive Live Map 🗺️**: Tap **Track Live Location** to view your rider's real-time GPS position moving along the route to your drop-off address.

---

## 7. Floating Chat Head & In-App Customer Support

- **Floating Chat Bubble**: When you have an active order, a floating chat head appears on screen for quick communication with your rider or station dispatcher.
- **Direct Notification Routing**: Tapping any chat or order update notification navigates you straight into the active conversation thread or order timeline!
- **Voice Call & SMS**: Tap the **Call** (`📞`) or **SMS** (`💬`) buttons on active order cards to reach your delivery rider immediately.

---

## 8. Digital E-Receipt & Order Invoicing

- Inside Order Details, tap **View Digital E-Receipt** to open an official summary modal with itemized pricing, delivery fees, payment method, order number (e.g. `#24`), and timestamps.
- **Save & Share**: Capture or export your receipt for expense filing and vehicle maintenance records.

---

## 9. Archiving & Managing Past Orders

Keep your order history screen organized:

- **Archive Order**: Tap **`Archive`** (`📦`) on any completed or cancelled order card to hide it from your active list.
- **Filter Tabs**: Switch between **`Active`** and **`Archived`** orders at the top of the history feed.
- **Restore Order**: Tap **`Restore`** (`↻`) on any archived order to move it back to your active list anytime.

---

## 10. Product Reviews & Feedback

1. Open the **Reviews** tab.
2. Select an eligible completed order.
3. Rate your product and rider experience (1 to 5 Stars ⭐).
4. Write comments or feedback and tap **Submit Review**.

---

## 11. Quick Troubleshooting & FAQ

- **Order items not refreshing**: Pull down on Order History to trigger a manual refresh, or check your internet connection.
- **1-Tap Re-Order failed**: Verify that the products from your previous order are currently active and in stock.
- **GPS pin is inaccurate**: Tap **Use Current Location** while outdoors, or manually drag the pin to your exact building entrance.
- **Push notifications not popping up**: Check your phone's Settings ➔ App Permissions ➔ Notifications and ensure permissions are set to Allowed.
